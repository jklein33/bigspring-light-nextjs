faqs:
  - title: What does Exodus Intelligence build?
    answer: Custom applications for small and mid-sized businesses. That includes websites, web apps, progressive web apps (PWAs), and AI solutions shaped to how the business already operates.

  - title: Who are these projects for?
    answer: Small and mid-sized businesses that need software of their own and do not want a large consulting engagement before anything is useful.

  - title: How does pricing work?
    answer: Work starts at a low startup cost. After the first version is in use, the application continues under a SaaS agreement instead of a large one-time development fee. Scope depends on the project. Contact Exodus Intelligence and we will talk through it.

  - title: What is the SaaS agreement?
    answer: A SaaS agreement means you use the software as an ongoing service. You are not funding a traditional build where a large fee is due before the application exists and every later change starts a new project.

  - title: What is a progressive web app?
    answer: A progressive web app is a website that behaves like an installed application. People open it in a browser and can add it to a phone or desktop home screen when that fits the work. It is a practical option when a separate app-store product is more than the business needs.

  - title: Do you only build AI?
    answer: No. AI is one service, alongside websites, web apps, progressive web apps, and other custom applications. AI is a fit when it takes a specific task out of the day-to-day work.

  - title: Can you build something off-the-shelf software does not cover?
    answer: Yes. The work is custom. We start from the problem in the business, then build the website, web app, progressive web app, or AI solution that fits that problem.

  - title: How do we get started?
    answer: Use the contact form or email james@exodusintelligence.ai and describe what you want the software to do.
